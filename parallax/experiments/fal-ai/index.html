Sky is a CSS gradient sampled from sky.png; it parallaxes via the sky row (background-position on the container). Clouds are their own layer (clouds.png, in front of the mountain for occlusion, anchored left bottom below 768px) and share the sky values to stay registered on the gradient. The foreground is trans-foreground-s85.png (the original translucent foreground; the solid/leveled experiments were cancelled and their files kept in this dir for later). Parallax math: each layer translates start + (end - start) * progress, where progress is the clamped scroll divided by the freeze point.
